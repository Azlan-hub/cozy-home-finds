## 2. Smooth Rolling Mobility

One of the biggest frustrations with traditional storage baskets is that once
they are full, they are heavy and impossible to drag around without scratching
the floor. 

This unit is equipped with smooth, heavy-duty caster wheels. This means you can
use it as a mobile sorting station. You can effortlessly roll it from the living
room to the bedroom when picking up clutter, or slide it out of the way when you
need to vacuum.

## 3. Durable & Timeless Design

Aesthetics matter just as much as function. The vintage-inspired, durable wire
design brings a subtle industrial yet cozy texture to your space. 

Unlike plastic storage bins that can crack or look cheap over time, a metal wire
basket holds its shape and fits seamlessly into almost any interior design
style—from mid-century modern to minimal Scandinavian. It intentionally puts
your neatly folded textiles and aesthetic books on display, turning your storage
into an actual piece of room decor.
